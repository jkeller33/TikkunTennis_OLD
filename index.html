Free tennis lessons · Two years and counting

Sharing the game with kids who deserve a chance to play.

Maya Keller is a nationally ranked junior tennis player. For the past two years she has volunteered her time and expertise teaching tennis, completely free, to Jewish kids and to any kids from disadvantaged backgrounds who might not otherwise get the chance to play.

Giving back

Free lessons from a nationally ranked player

Lessons, court time and equipment add up quickly, and too many kids never get their first lesson. Maya has spent the last two years changing that. She shares the same skills she uses in national competition with kids in the Jewish community and with any kids from disadvantaged backgrounds who want to learn.

There is no cost to families, ever. Kids just need to show up ready to have fun and learn.
